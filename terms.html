1. Purpose and scope

Moneta helps you organize personal transactions, budgets, savings goals and manually recorded or explicitly linked holdings. It provides calculations and estimates, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Moneta is not a bank, a licensed financial institution or a regulated banking provider. It does not hold funds, execute transfers or provide investment services.

2. Responsible use

Use only financial information you are authorized to process. Keep your device, backups and Moneta banking credentials secure. Do not attempt to access another person’s records, bypass authentication or misuse the bank integration. The existing financial workspace is stored locally and is accessible to someone with access to that browser profile.

These terms do not grant access to any bank or permission to offer unrestricted banking access to the public.

The implemented integration reads account information, balances and history. It does not initiate payments. You authenticate with the bank outside Moneta. Provider and bank terms also apply; see Enable Banking’s terms and the notices presented during authorization. You may disconnect a bank or revoke authorization through your bank. Previously imported local data is removed separately.
